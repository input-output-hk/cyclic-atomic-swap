# CANS Protocol - Weekly Report: 2nd March – 8th March 2026

## Progress

1. Mr. Judge and Luca completed the draft of the distributed state machine representing the General Cyclic Multi-Party Atomic Swap (GAS) Protocol.
   1. Published at btcdefi-lpqp/tree/ldebiasi-n-swap (private repo)
   2. PDF of the UML Activity/State view is attached for your convenience.
   3. The GAS protocol is based on the distributed state machine theoretical model, which imagines parallel and concurrent processes running on different computational nodes, exchanging messages across a network, and supporting temporary partitions and disconnections.
   4. This is an event-driven architectural model.
   5. Internal timeout watchdogs at the joining barriers, which occur when processes wait for messages, are not yet represented except when mentioned in the protocol description. Those will be represented when we will design the implementation architecture.
   6. "Refund" and "the leader soliciting secrets" sub-activities are in elaboration.
   7. The GAS architecture can now be imagined within Cardano.
